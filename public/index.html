Does today look like a usual day?

Hearthbeat answers that one question for families, using only when a Ring device noticed movement. It never opens video, audio or snapshots.

  1. 1Learns their usual day from the last two weeks.
  2. 2Compares today with it, as the day happens.
  3. 3Tells you gently, and shows exactly why.
